Journalist trades the newsroom for the brand room, keeps the nose for news.

From India Today's political desk to PR for PhonePe and redBus, she knows what makes an editor say yes.

By the Editorial Desk · Bengaluru

Meghna Barik is a communications and public relations professional whose career runs through agency PR, corporate communications, journalism and long-form content. She has driven media relations, strategic storytelling, thought leadership and earned media visibility for leading fintech and consumer brands.

Before crossing over to PR, she wrote 9–10 news stories a day as a sub-editor at India Today, filed bylines for The Hindu and The Hindu BusinessLine, and interviewed CEOs for exclusive insight.

That reporter's training is the edge: she pitches like someone who has sat on the other side of the inbox. Her work spans business, technology, fintech and consumer sectors, combining media outreach, reputation management and research-driven communication strategy.

Off the clock, she is a trained Odissi dancer with national and international honours, a three-time Best Delegate at Model UN, and a four-time oratory and debate winner.
